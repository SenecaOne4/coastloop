How CoastLoop works

Everything below follows this same path. You control the network; brokers only work the sales side.

1 · ProspectYou or a broker adds a local business lead.
2 · CustomerPromote it as Host, Advertiser, or Both.
3 · DealHosts receive a TV placement offer. Advertisers buy campaign time.
4 · DeliveryTVs report real playback and CoastLoop shows the economics.
