Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CoastLoop collects, uses, discloses, and retains information when people use CoastLoop websites, accounts, advertising services, Host services, support channels, and the CoastLoop Roku application.

1. Scope

CoastLoop is a business-focused digital advertising and screen-management service. At launch, CoastLoop does not use participating-screen cameras, facial recognition, biometric identification, or person-level audience measurement. Proof-of-Play is device-level delivery telemetry, not proof that a person viewed an advertisement.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CoastLoop, we may collect account and contact information; business, advertiser, campaign, Host, venue, and screen information; order, billing, payment-status, and limited payment-method metadata; uploaded or generated creative and approvals; support communications; and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

Participating Roku and other supported devices may provide screen or device identifiers, device model, software or application version, connectivity state, timestamps, playlist and campaign assignments, playback events, durations or completion indicators where recorded, errors, diagnostics, and service-health information. Website and service requests may also provide Internet Protocol address, browser or device type, requested features, security logs, and similar technical information.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and secure accounts; pair, operate, maintain, and troubleshoot screens; create, review, schedule, and deliver advertising; produce device-level Proof-of-Play and delivery reporting; process orders and Host participation; support payments and lawful tax reporting; communicate about service, legal terms, security, and support; prevent fraud and abuse; enforce agreements and policies; comply with law; and improve service reliability and features.

4. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information to service providers that support hosting, storage, authentication, payments, email, communications, security, creative production, and other CoastLoop operations; to Advertisers or Hosts when necessary to perform the applicable campaign or venue relationship; to professional advisers; to authorities or other persons when required or reasonably necessary by law; and in connection with a business reorganization or transfer, subject to applicable law.

CoastLoop does not sell personal information for money. CoastLoop launch screens do not use camera-based or person-level audience tracking for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Proof-of-Play

Proof-of-Play indicates device-reported advertising playback. It does not establish that any particular person was present or saw, heard, noticed, understood, or engaged with the advertising.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

CoastLoop may use cookies, browser storage, and similar technologies for authentication, session continuity, security, fraud prevention, preferences, and service functionality.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for periods reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business and tax records, prove contract acceptance and campaign delivery, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and comply with legal holds or other applicable requirements.

8. Security

CoastLoop uses administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information appropriate to its nature and risk. No security program can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices and Requests

You may request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights where applicable by contacting CoastLoop. We may need to verify identity and may retain information where law or legitimate recordkeeping obligations require it.

10. Children

The CoastLoop service is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. CoastLoop does not knowingly seek to collect personal information online from children under 13.

11. State-Specific Rights

Residents of some U.S. states may have additional privacy rights. CoastLoop will honor rights applicable to it in the relevant jurisdiction.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as our service, vendors, legal requirements, or data practices change. The current version will state its effective date.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and legally recognized privacy requests: privacy@coastloop.site

General support: support@coastloop.site
