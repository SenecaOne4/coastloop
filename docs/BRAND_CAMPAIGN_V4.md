# CoastLoop Brand + House Campaign V4

## CEO direction

This is the final pre-launch brand/house campaign pass.

Production imagery is LOCAL-ONLY through Seneca's local generator.
Never use ChatGPT/cloud image generation for CoastLoop production artwork.

## Brand position

CoastLoop should feel:
- luxury
- cinematic
- editorial
- architectural
- expensive
- restrained
- unmistakably local without looking like "local advertising"

Visual language:
- black marble
- warm brass/champagne gold
- deep wine accents
- ivory typography
- warm practical lighting
- premium hospitality / retail / lifestyle environments
- generous negative space
- deliberate composition
- no generic ad-template aesthetic

## Logo

Do NOT redesign the CoastLoop mark.

Give the existing mark only a micro-lift:
- slightly richer contrast
- slightly warmer gold presence
- extremely subtle depth/glow
- no geometry changes
- no new symbol
- no heavy bevel
- no gimmick effects

The existing logo remains the logo.

## Asset policy

4K master is the creative source of truth.

Older prototypes / low-resolution authored creative are legacy and must not
represent the current CoastLoop brand.

Lower-resolution technical delivery transcodes may exist only as invisible
compatibility derivatives. They are not canonical brand assets.

## Final approval gate

Do not replace production House inventory until:
1. all three new local source plates are reviewed;
2. deterministic typography is composited;
3. storyboard is approved;
4. 4K motion master is reviewed;
5. actual player delivery is verified.
