- **Multi-location same business, same zone:** no per-location charge — one business, one campaign, one zone. (Locations are hosts, not buyers.)
- **Additional distinct campaign** (different offer/season, same zone): **$350** per campaign produced (creative allocation $250 + margin), or free via the add-on's refreshes if the variant replaces the running one.

### Screen-HOST pricing — its own ledger (management-directed section)
**Recommendation: hosts are PAID for verified performance, never charged, and pay is earned against the ledger — not promised flat.**

| Host term | Offer |
|---|---|
| Placement fee charged to host | **$0 — deliberately not priced.** Distribution is the scarce side; charging hosts kills supply. Host also pays $0 for equipment — there is none (their TV, our channel/player; NO additional hardware is the point). |
| Founding Host (first 5 signed locations) | **$300/screen/year**, paid annually in arrears |
| Standard Host | **$200/screen/year**, paid annually in arrears (schema cap is $599 — headroom exists, not used) |
| **Payment condition (the load-bearing term)** | Pay is earned only by screens averaging **≥8 verified hours/day** over the period with sleep alarms investigated — measured by `playback_ledger.py`. A screen that sleeps does not earn. Pro-rate below threshold; $0 for a screen dark >30 days. |
| Host's own promotion | 1 in-loop house slot for the venue's own promo, creative produced once/yr included — this is in-kind value worth the $1,000 advertiser price if bought, and it costs us one creative allocation. |
| Host who also advertises | Standard advertiser price **minus** their host pay as a credit (netting — less cash moves, same margin). |

Why pay hosts at all: a paid, verified-hours contract is what makes a venue keep the screen on, sleep-proofed, and in a good position — it buys *placement quality and uptime*, which is what advertisers are actually paying for. $200–$300/screen/yr is 20–30% of ONE advertiser's annual fee; at target density (below) it dilutes to ~$200 per advertiser.

## 2. Margin math — what CoastLoop KEEPS (profit, not revenue)
Per advertiser, per year, at target zone density (10 screens / 10 advertisers in zone → host cost $200/advertiser at Standard Host rate; Founding-era density will be thinner and host cost per advertiser higher — flagged, not hidden):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price collected | $1,000 | $1,000 | $1,500 | $1,500 |
| Broker 15% (on collected only) | $0 | −$150 | $0 | −$225 |

Add-on ($100/mo = $1,200/yr): delivery cost = 2 refreshes × $150 + reporting ~$25 = −$325; broker 15% applies **only if the broker sourced the add-on, first year only** (−$180). **Net kept: $875 direct (73%) / $695 brokered (58%).** The add-on is the highest-margin product we have — because its deliverables are automated or already built (ledger reporting, delivery_weight).

Monthly restructured (first year $2,050): brokered at 15% on collected = −$308; creative −$250; setup covers onboarding; collections cost quarterly ≈ −$67 (see §5); host share −$200. **Net ≈ $1,225 direct / $917 brokered** — monthly buyers pay for the privilege of paying slowly, as they should.

**Cash reality check:** the allocations above are mostly internal/opportunity cost. Actual cash out per direct Founding Annual is close to $0–$50 (host pay is the only scheduled cash, paid in arrears after verified performance). **Cash margin on a direct annual sale is ~95%+ in year one.** That is the Round-2 profit shape Seneca asked for — provided creative capacity is not given away below allocation (monthly defect, fixed above) and host pay stays performance-gated.

## 3. Portfolio money questions — answered head-on (one treatment each)
1. **Owned businesses (GC Help, OpenPair, BTCBB, HotBatch, Children In Pakistan, Ghost Go, DashLens): PURE HOUSE INVENTORY — $0 internal charge, no fake revenue on CoastLoop's books.** Each portfolio campaign carries a *shadow line* of $250 creative allocation in that business's own tracking, so nobody mistakes free for costless. CoastLoop reports paid plays and portfolio plays separately, always.
2. **Slot priority: PAYING CLIENTS FIRST.** Paid campaigns hold guaranteed standard weight; the CoastLoop house slot (1, permanent — it is the demo) stays; portfolio campaigns fill unsold loop capacity **capped at 25% of loop time**, and portfolio is bumped first when paid demand grows. Adopting this now costs nothing — the loop has unsold capacity today (Nov 20 playlist cut).
3. **Broker share when nobody brokered: IT DOES NOT EXIST.** No broker, no 15% — CoastLoop keeps 100%. It does not accrue to a pool or to Seneca-as-broker by default. Exception, stated once: when Seneca personally sources an *external* advertiser, he is broker of record at 15% (already his profile) and is paid like any broker — that is real sourcing work. On *portfolio* deals there is no broker share, including for Seneca.

## 4. Runner-up (Structure B) — simpler, thinner

## 5. Monthly on a manual rail — the honest costing (feeds management's rail paper)
Admin time valued at $40/hr. Per manual payment: ~5 min verify Cash App + ~5 min record/reconcile + chase time amortized.

| Billing cadence | Touches/yr | Admin time | Labor cost | As % of gross |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Annual prepay ($1,000) | 1 | ~15 min | **~$10** | 1% |
| Quarterly ($450/qtr) | 4 | ~80 min | **~$53–67** | ~3–4% |
| Monthly manual ($100/mo) | 12 | ~4 hrs incl. chases | **~$160** | **13% of $1,200** |

Plus default risk: on manual monthly, a buyer who defaults at month 4 has consumed the full creative production for $400 collected. **Plain statement for the rail paper: true monthly billing only works with a recurring-capable rail. On Cash App manual, the sellable forms are annual prepay and quarterly billing. This proposal is built that way, and no pricing in it requires a rail change.**
