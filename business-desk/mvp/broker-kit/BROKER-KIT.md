## What a broker is selling (one paragraph, approved language)
CoastLoop puts a local business on the TV screens inside other local businesses — spas, restaurants, waiting rooms, bars — across the Grand Strand. CoastLoop *creates* the 15-second ad to a luxury standard, delivers it to commissioned screens, and proves every play: playback is recorded per screen, per play. No additional hardware for anyone — venues use their own TV, advertisers never touch equipment. The network is vetted: not every business gets on.

## Broker economics — exactly how you get paid
- **15% of collected cash** on every campaign you source. Commission is calculated on money *collected*, never on bookings or invoices. Example: $1,000 annual collected → **$150 earned**. Flagship $2,999 → **$450 earned**.
- Your rate is snapshotted onto each campaign when it's created — a later rate change never rewrites an old campaign.
- Your Broker Desk shows three numbers, always current: **Earned** (on collected cash), **Scheduled** (payouts created/approved, in flight), **Paid**. Owed to you = Earned − Paid − Scheduled. No double-pay is possible; the system blocks over-payment.
- Payouts are manual at launch (Cash App or bank, per the payment details on your broker profile) and recorded in your Desk with a payment reference. Status flow: due → approved → paid. You get a notification at every step, and an email when a payout is marked paid.
- **Set your payment details first:** Broker Desk → "How I get paid" (Cash App handle / bank reference). No details = no payout can be sent.

## How a broker works the Desk (the journey, all live and proven Nov 11)
1. You receive an invite email from **hello@mail.coastloop.site** (check Promotions/Spam the first time; add us to contacts). The invite **expires in 14 days**.
2. Accept via the GENNYAuth sign-in link in the invite. Your Broker Desk opens at coastloop.site/broker.
3. Add prospects (businesses you're working). Promote a prospect when they're ready — it becomes a business in your book, attributed to you.
4. Create the campaign (your 15% snapshots automatically). You can activate your own scheduled campaigns, and edit/delete your own drafts within the Desk rules.
5. CoastLoop issues the invoice; the advertiser pays (Cash App, manual activation at launch). Your commission appears as *earned* the moment cash is collected — not before.
6. Watch your money in the Desk; payouts land per the schedule above.

## What a broker can never see (say it plainly — it's a trust feature)
Other brokers' books, CoastLoop's margins or creative costs, portfolio/internal campaign economics, admin functions, and any venue's or advertiser's terms beyond your own deals. Your book is yours; the scoping is enforced by the system, and it was tested with a second broker account — it held.

## Talk track — do / don't
**Do:** lead with proof ("every play recorded, by screen"), the created-for-you creative ("you approve it before it ever plays"), the founding lock (price held at first renewal), and no-hardware simplicity.
**Don't:** promise play counts or screen counts that aren't in writing (current packages don't guarantee a number — sell verified proof, not projections); promise monthly card billing; promise a launch date tighter than "days, typically 1–3 from approval of the creative direction" (rendering is a real craft step, one screen-ready ad at a time); offer discounts — there are none outside the flagship bundle; use Seneca's personal network or name-drop relationships you don't personally hold.

## FAQ (broker-facing)
- *When do I get paid on a deal?* Commission earns when the advertiser's cash is collected; payouts are then created, approved, and paid manually with a reference you can see.
- *What if my client pays late or partially?* You earn 15% of what is actually collected, as it is collected.
- *Can I bring a venue (screen host) instead of an advertiser?* Yes — tell us; hosts are a separate track (venues are paid for verified uptime, never charged). Host deals are credited to your book as sourced relationships; host terms are set by CoastLoop directly.
- *Who makes the ad?* CoastLoop, always. Clients approve before launch; they don't supply finished ads.
- *What does the advertiser actually get shown as proof?* Verified plays recorded by screen, reported monthly (screen-level verified-hours detail on the add-on/flagship).
