# CoastLoop Venue (Host) Kit — MVP-presentable (Nov 20, 2026)
**Status: Structure A APPROVED Nov 20, 2026. Host pay figures below are the approved structure's terms, held as proposed (the pricing decision did not separately speak to host pay — flagged to management). Nothing is sent to any venue until Seneca says yes (outreach gate unchanged).**

## The host offer in one paragraph
Your TV, earning its wall space. CoastLoop runs a premium, vetted loop of local advertising on a screen you already own — **no additional hardware, no box to buy, nothing to install beyond the CoastLoop channel/player**. You approve the exact screen, schedule, house content, and category exclusions before anything goes live. In return, CoastLoop **pays you** for verified uptime — measured by the same playback ledger that proves plays to advertisers.

## Host terms (Structure A, approved Nov 20, 2026)
- **Placement fee charged to you: $0. Equipment cost: $0.** There is no equipment.
- **Founding Host (first 5 signed locations): $300 per screen per year.**
- **Standard Host: $200 per screen per year.**
- Paid **annually in arrears** — because pay is earned on performance, measured, not promised:
  - Your screen must average **≥8 verified hours of playback per day** over the period.
  - Playback is proven by CoastLoop's verified-hours ledger (the player reports every completed play; hours are summed server-side).
  - A screen that sleeps doesn't earn for the time it's dark. Below-threshold performance pro-rates; a screen dark more than 30 days earns $0 for that period.
- **Your own promotion:** one slot in the loop for your venue's own promo, with the creative produced for you once a year, included. (As an advertiser product that's the $1,000 founding offer — as a host, it's in-kind.)
- **Host + advertiser:** if you also advertise your business on the network, your host pay is credited against your advertiser price. Less cash moves; same value.
- **Control stays yours:** placement, operating mode, and commercial terms are approved by you before install; category exclusions honored (a competitor's ad never plays on your screen if you've excluded the category).

## Install — what actually happens (venue install checklist, proven Nov 20 on the house TCL)
1. We confirm the screen and its position with you, and agree operating hours.
2. Sleep-proofing (one-time, five TV settings — sleep timer off, auto power savings off, fast start on, screensaver off, bandwidth saver off). A CoastLoop screen never sleeps; these settings are the whole trick, and we verify them with you.
3. Launch CoastLoop (Roku channel or web player), pair the screen by code, attach your playlist.
4. **Overnight proof:** the screen plays overnight and the ledger verifies continuous receipts. Any gap over 15 minutes flags the install as incomplete — we fix it before the install is called done.
5. From there it runs itself: playback, proof, and reporting are automatic and continue even if our studio computers are off.

## What you will see as a host
- Your screen's verified hours (the same ledger number your pay is based on — no black box).
- What's playing on your screen (your playlist), including your own promo slot.
- Your host payout status: due → approved → paid, with a payment reference.

## What we ask of you (the whole list)
Keep the screen on during agreed hours, don't cover or move it without telling us, keep the TV's internet connected, and tell us if the screen is damaged or replaced. That's it — content, creative, advertisers, and proof are CoastLoop's job.

## FAQ (host-facing)
- *Does it cost me anything?* No fee, no hardware. Your existing TV and internet.
- *Can I say no to an advertiser?* Yes — category exclusions are set before install and honored by the playlist system.
- *How is my pay calculated, really?* Verified playback hours from the ledger, against the ≥8 hrs/day standard, paid annually. You can see the same numbers we pay on.
- *What plays besides ads?* CoastLoop house pieces and THE GALA art library between campaigns — the loop is designed to look premium in your room, not like a billboard that wandered indoors.
- *Can I advertise my own business to other venues?* Yes — that's the advertiser track; your host pay credits against it.
